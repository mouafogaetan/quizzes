Grammar: Personal Pronouns + Verb "To Be"

Personal Pronouns

  • I - first person singular
  • You - second person singular/plural
  • He - third person singular (masculine)
  • She - third person singular (feminine)
  • It - third person singular (neutral/object)
  • We - first person plural
  • They - third person plural

Verb "To Be" - Present Simple

Pronoun Verb "To Be" Example
I am I am a student.
You are You are French.
He/She/It is She is 25 years old.
We are We are friends.
They are They are from Spain.

Contractions

  • I am → I'm
  • You are → You're
  • He is → He's
  • She is → She's
  • It is → It's
  • We are → We're
  • They are → They're

Phonology: Rising Intonation in Yes/No Questions

Rising Intonation

In English, yes/no questions typically end with a rising intonation. This means your voice goes up at the end of the question.

Examples:

  • "Are you French?" /ɑːr juː frɛntʃ ↗/
  • "Is she a student?" /ɪz ʃiː ə ˈstjuːdənt ↗/
  • "Do you like coffee?" /duː juː laɪk ˈkɒfi ↗/

Visual Representation:

Are you French?

→ ↗

(Voice rises at the end)

Exercise 3: Identify the Rising Intonation

Which of these questions should have a rising intonation?

1. What is your name?

2. Are you from Italy?

3. How old are you?

4. Is he a teacher?

5. Where are you from?

Questions with rising intonation: 2 and 4

Explanation: Yes/no questions (questions that can be answered with "yes" or "no") have rising intonation. Information questions (what, where, how, etc.) typically have falling intonation.

Oral Expression: Short Introduction Dialogues

Basic Introduction Dialogue

Person A: Hello, my name is Sarah. What's your name?

Person B: Hi Sarah, I'm Mark. Nice to meet you.

Person A: Nice to meet you too, Mark. Where are you from?

Person B: I'm from Canada. And you?

Person A: I'm from Australia. How old are you?

Person B: I'm 28 years old. What about you?

Person A: I'm 25. Well, it was nice meeting you!

Person B: You too! See you later!

Introducing Someone Else

Person A: Hi John, this is my friend Maria.

Person B: Hello Maria, nice to meet you. I'm John.

Person C: Nice to meet you too, John.

Person A: Maria is from Spain, and she's a student.

Person B: That's interesting. What are you studying?

Person C: I'm studying English literature.
